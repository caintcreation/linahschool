About Linah Academy

Quest for Excellence in Early Childhood Education

Linah Academy is a premier early childhood education center dedicated to providing a nurturing and stimulating environment where children can grow, learn, and thrive.

Founded on the principle that every child deserves the best start in life, we combine innovative teaching methods with a caring approach to help children develop socially, emotionally, and academically.

Our experienced educators create engaging learning experiences that spark curiosity and foster a lifelong love of learning. We believe that joyful foundational years build confident, resilient, and compassionate lifelong achievers.

Nurturing Environment

We provide a safe, caring atmosphere where children feel valued, respected, and warmly supported every day.

Quality Education

Our curriculum is thoughtfully structured to stimulate intellectual growth, cognitive skills, and foundational literacy.

Community Focus

We build strong, genuine partnerships with families to support and celebrate each child's individual developmental journey.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information for parents considering admission at Linah Academy.

We operate from Monday to Friday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and on Saturdays from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We offer both half-day and full-day schedules to accommodate family needs and busy work schedules.

We enroll children aged 2 through 5 across three distinct early childhood levels:

  • Playgroup: Ages 2 to 3 years
  • PP1 (Pre-Primary 1): Ages 3 to 4 years
  • PP2 (Pre-Primary 2): Ages 4 to 5 years

Linah Academy is conveniently situated in Kikuyu Town, right next to the Kikuyu Law Courts in Kiambu County. Parents are warmly welcomed to book a campus tour between Monday and Saturday.

We follow Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) guidelines infused with play-based, experiential learning methodologies. This balances early foundational literacy and numeracy with creative expression, social manners, and critical thinking.
