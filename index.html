About Linah Academy

Quest for Excellence

Linah Academy is a premier early childhood education center dedicated to providing a nurturing and stimulating environment where children can grow, learn, and thrive.

Founded on the principle that every child deserves the best start in life, we combine innovative teaching methods with a caring approach to help children develop socially, emotionally, and academically.

Our experienced educators create engaging learning experiences that spark curiosity and foster a lifelong love of learning.

Our Programs

Playgroup

Ages: 2-3 years

Our playgroup program focuses on socialization, sensory exploration, and early learning through play-based activities.

  • Creative play and exploration
  • Basic social skills development
  • Early language and numeracy
  • Motor skills enhancement
Learn More

PP1

Ages: 3-4 years

Our PP1 program builds foundational skills in literacy, numeracy, and critical thinking through structured play.

  • Introduction to letters and numbers
  • Problem-solving activities
  • Creative expression
  • Social and emotional development
Learn More

PP2

Ages: 4-5 years

Our PP2 program prepares children for primary school with advanced literacy, numeracy, and independent learning skills.

  • Reading and writing readiness
  • Mathematical concepts
  • Science exploration
  • Confidence and independence building
